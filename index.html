SEO & Growth

Websites that rank, not just look good.

SEO — search engine optimization — is how Google decides which local businesses show up first when someone searches “contractor near me” or “home remodel in [your city].” A great-looking site that nobody finds doesn’t grow your business. Ranking higher does: it means more of the homeowners already searching for what you do end up on your site instead of a competitor’s.

That’s why our Premium package builds SEO in as an ongoing service, not a one-time setup. We handle local keyword targeting, on-page optimization, and monthly search visibility tracking — so your site keeps climbing the rankings and keeps earning leads long after launch, instead of sitting still while competitors outrank you.

  • Show up when homeowners are actively searching — not just browsing
  • Outrank local competitors for the services you actually offer

SEO in our packages

What's the difference between Pro and Premium SEO?

Pro gets your site set up correctly so Google can find and understand it. Premium goes after rankings: we research what your customers search for and build every page to compete for those searches.

Pro · One-time setup

Basic on-page SEO setup

The technical foundation, done once at launch. Your site is built so Google can crawl it, index it, and understand what each page is about.

  • Clean page structure with proper headings on every page
  • Page titles that include your business name and main service
  • Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console so your pages get indexed
  • Fast, mobile-friendly pages with a secure (https) connection

Gets you search-ready. It doesn't target specific keywords or chase rankings.

Premium

Local SEO optimization & keyword research

We find the exact phrases homeowners in your area type into Google, like “roof repair Daytona Beach,” and build your site to rank for them.

  • Keyword research for your services and the towns you serve
  • A target keyword assigned to each page
  • Service-area and city pages so you show up beyond your home town
  • Consistent business name, address & phone, plus local business data Google reads
Premium

SEO titles, descriptions & image optimization

What people see in search results decides whether they click. We write it for every page, and tune your images so pages stay fast.

  • A custom title and description for each page, written around its keyword
  • Images compressed and resized so pages load quickly on phones
  • Descriptive file names and alt text so images can show up in Google Images
Premium · Monthly

Ongoing SEO maintenance

Rankings aren't set-and-forget. Each month we track where you show up and keep improving the pages that matter.

  • Monthly search visibility tracking
  • Updates to pages and keywords as your rankings move
  • New local pages as you add services or service areas
