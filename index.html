Questions

Good to know before we start.

How long does a project take? +

Most projects are completed within 7–10 days from our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback on the design.

Do I need to already have a domain or hosting? +

No — we can help you register a domain and set up hosting from scratch, or work with what you already have.

Can you write the content for me? +

Yes. Every website includes copywriting. We learn about your business and services, then turn that information into clear, professional content built to represent your company and set you apart.

What's the difference between the monthly service plans? +

The Pro plan ($100/mo, 1-year commitment) covers hosting, security and software updates, uptime monitoring, and small content edits — everything needed to keep your site running smoothly. The Premium plan ($200/mo, 1-year commitment) includes all of that plus ongoing local SEO work: keyword research, on-page optimization, and priority support. The Basic package has no monthly plan — you own the site outright with nothing recurring.

What's covered under the warranty? +

Any bug, broken link, or page that isn't working the way it was scoped gets fixed at no charge — it covers our workmanship, not new features or scope changes. For Basic, that coverage runs 30 days after launch. For Pro and Premium, bug fixes are included for as long as your service plan is active, since that's part of what the monthly plan covers.

What happens if I decide not to renew my service plan? +

Your website and domain remain yours. If you choose not to renew after your initial service term, we'll help transition your website and domain management to you or your new provider. After the transition, you'll be responsible for hosting, domain renewals, maintenance, and any ongoing services previously provided by NSD Digital.

Do you only work with local businesses? +

We specialize in websites for contractors, but we work with businesses across other industries too. If you need a professional website, we can help.
