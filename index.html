Domain Registration & Management

Every NSD Digital website includes domain registration and setup. If you don't already own a domain, we'll help you select, register, and connect one standard domain name — and we cover domain registration costs up to $25 per year. Any cost above $25, including premium, aftermarket, or specialty domains, is the client's responsibility. If you already own a domain, we'll connect and configure your existing domain as part of the website setup.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common questions about our websites and service plans.

How long does it take to build a website? +

Most projects are completed within 7–10 days from our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback on the design.

Do you work with businesses outside our area? +

We specialize in websites for contractors, but we work with businesses across other industries too. If you need a professional website, we can help.

What's included in the ongoing support? +

Pro and Premium service plans include hosting, security and software updates, uptime monitoring, and small content edits — everything needed to keep your site running smoothly.

Will my website be mobile friendly? +

Yes — every website we build is mobile-first and tested across devices before launch.

Do you help with SEO? +

Yes. Every build includes basic on-page SEO setup, and Premium includes ongoing local SEO and keyword research.

Can I update the content myself? +

Yes — we'll walk you through how to make simple updates yourself, or your service plan can include us handling changes for you.
