Questions

Good to know before we start.

How long does a project take? +

Most projects are completed within 1 week from our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback on the design.

Do I need to already have a domain or hosting? +

No — we can help you register a domain and set up hosting from scratch, or work with what you already have.

Can you write the content for me? +

Yes. Every build includes copywriting — we'll ask you a short set of questions about your business and write pages built around clear, honest answers rather than generic filler.

What's the difference between the monthly service plans? +

The Pro plan ($75/mo) covers hosting, security and software updates, uptime monitoring, and small content edits — everything needed to keep your site running smoothly. The Premium plan ($125/mo) includes all of that plus ongoing SEO work: keyword research, on-page optimization, and priority support. The Basic package has no monthly plan — you own the site outright with nothing recurring.

What's covered under the warranty? +

For 30 days after launch, any bug, broken link, or page that isn't working the way it was scoped gets fixed at no charge. It covers our workmanship — it doesn't cover new features or scope changes.

Do you only work with local businesses? +

That's our focus, but not a hard rule — if your project fits how we work, we're happy to talk it through.
